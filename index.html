Akihabara main building

at-home cafe Akihabara Main

The best-known maid café in Akihabara, on several floors of one building. Children aged 6 and under get a kids card and a free kids drink.

Monday 11:00 to 22:00
Seat charge: ¥850 per adult, ¥400 for ages 13 to 18, free for ages 12 and under (official site). One drink per person. You stay about an hour, and a longer stay costs nothing extra.

Maidreamin, 6F

maidreamin Akihabara Main Store

A big chain that started in Akihabara. Its FAQ says families with children are welcome, with table seats recommended. Children up to sixth grade get a free ice cream.

Monday 11:30 to 23:00
Seat charge, per hour: ¥880 per adult, ¥550 for junior and senior high school, ¥330 for elementary school, free for younger children (official site). It is charged again for each extra hour.
